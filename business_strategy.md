# Business Strategy – Reliable Corporate Transfers

## 1. LinkedIn – Focus on Companies

**Goal:** HR managers, travel managers, small and medium-sized businesses.  
**Reason:** Decision-makers can immediately see your reliability and transparent pricing.

### Strategy

- **Profile Optimization**
  - Tagline: *“Reliable Corporate Transfers – Guaranteed On-Time, Transparent Pricing”*
  - Profile photo: professional logo or vehicle
  - Banner: *“Corporate Transfers | Schiphol & Business Routes”*

- **Content Plan**
  1. **Case Studies**  
     - Example: *“5 employees transferred from Schiphol to Amsterdam offices on time. 100% on schedule.”*
  2. **Feature Highlights**  
     - *“15 minutes free wait time, fair pricing, optional water bottle for comfort – from the very first ride.”*
  3. **Pricing Transparency**  
     - Show your **Comfort / Van pricing table** as an infographic or PDF download for HR managers.
  4. **Testimonials (later)**  
     - Collect feedback once you have repeat clients.

- **Connections**
  - Target HR/travel managers using LinkedIn Sales Navigator
  - Short personal message:  
    *“Hi [Name], we provide reliable corporate transfers in Amsterdam with transparent pricing. Would love to discuss how we can make employee travel stress-free.”*

- **Engagement**
  - Comment on posts about business travel, expat management, or office mobility.
  - Share content from HR communities, adding your insights.

---

## 2. Partnerships

- Hotels, coworking spaces, expat services
- Travel management companies
- Event organizers (conferences, trainings, team trips)
- **Pitch:** *“Reliable transfers for your clients/employees with predictable pricing, on-time guarantee.”*

---

## 3. SEO / Search Engine Ads

**Target:** companies, expats, families who value certainty.

- **SEO Keywords**
  - “Corporate airport transfer Amsterdam”
  - “Employee shuttle Schiphol”
  - “Reliable airport taxi Netherlands”

- **Ads (Google / Facebook / Instagram)**
  - Focus on *“book in advance / guaranteed transfer”* for families and expats
  - Target by location and interests: expats, professionals, families in the Amsterdam region

---

## 4. Messaging / Differentiation

- **Promise:** *“From the very first ride – guaranteed on-time, predictable pricing, professional service.”*  
- **Extra Comfort:** 15 minutes free wait, optional water bottle (highlight on booking page, optional for the client)  
- **USP:** Positioned just below high-end market – reliability, certainty, transparent pricing without premium luxury costs

---

## 5. Pricing Table – Indicative Ride Prices

| Distance   | Comfort (€) | Van (€) |
| ---------- | ----------- | ------- |
| 1 – 5 km   | 25          | 30      |
| 6 – 10 km  | 27 – 33     | 33 – 42 |
| 11 – 15 km | 35 – 43     | 44 – 53 |
| 16 – 20 km | 44 – 54     | 57 – 69 |
| 21 – 30 km | 57 – 79     | 72 – 99 |

> Note: Prices are indicative for advance bookings. Fares are calculated based on base fare + distance + time for rides under 15 km. Over 15 km, time fare is excluded.

## 6. Timing / Priorities

1. **LinkedIn + Partnerships** → immediate relevance for corporate clients  
2. **SEO + Google Ads** → later, target families and expats  
3. **Reviews & Testimonials** → collect to build credibility and social proof

---

## 7. Optional Add-Ons for Clients

- 15 minutes free wait time included  
- Optional water bottle for comfort (highlighted on booking page only)  

---

## 8. Target Customers

- Companies sending employees to/from Schiphol or other business destinations  
- Families or individuals who value reliability and punctuality  
- Not targeting Uber/Bolt-style price-sensitive spontaneous rides at this stage  

---

## 9. Marketing Focus

- LinkedIn: corporate clients, HR & travel managers  
- Partnerships: hotels, coworking, expat services, event organizers  
- Ads: Google/Facebook for families and expats looking for certainty  
- Messaging: reliability, transparency, comfort, on-time guarantee
